About

I'm Jomari, a freelancer from Alagar, Dalipuga, Iligan City. I split my time between design and operations: I make the posts, promos and posters a brand needs, and I also handle the behind-the-scenes work like product uploads, store updates, inboxes and calendars.

Having both skills means I can take a campaign from the first sketch to a live Shopify listing without handing it off. I work with fashion, fitness and church/community brands, and I like clear layouts, strong type and deadlines that are met.

05

How I Manage Content

  1. 01

    Brief & goals

    We agree on the offer, audience, brand look and what success means for the month.

  2. 02

    Plan the calendar

    I map posts by content type and date so the feed has a mix of promos, products and engagement.

  3. 03

    Design in batches

    Posts are designed a week at a time using one template system, so the feed stays consistent.

  4. 04

    Approval

    Drafts go to the client in one folder with clear file names. Revisions are tracked in one place.

  5. 05

    Schedule & post

    Approved posts are scheduled with captions and hashtags, and store listings are updated to match.

  6. 06

    Review

    At month end I check what worked and use it to shape the next calendar.
